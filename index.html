Retro Bomber Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Retro Bomber does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration of our own.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • The game itself runs entirely on your device.
  • Your high score is saved on your device and deleted when you delete the app.
  • If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your final score is posted to the game's Game Center leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the developer never receives your Apple account or personal details. You can turn Game Center off in Settings.
  • The optional "Buy me a coffee" tip is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; the developer receives no payment or personal details.
